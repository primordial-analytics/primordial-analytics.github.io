A small team, glad to be here.

Primordial Analytics was incorporated on August 9, 2018. We began as a small group of data scientists, engineers, and AI experts doing client work for people who already knew us as individuals.

In 2024 we turned our attention to volunteer data science for non-profit community health organizations, and we kept serving our longtime clients on a smaller scale.

This has been a hard year for the community health organizations we exist to serve. Fewer people asked for our help, which makes sense: even free analytics costs the hours of a person to explain the data and act on it. We wrote about it on our eighth anniversary.

How we try to work

Small is fine

A single chart, a spreadsheet check, or a short call can beat a big project that no one has time to host.

Share what we build

We would like busy teams to be able to pick up what we make on a Tuesday afternoon, without a kickoff meeting.

No pressure

If your answer is “not right now,” that is the right answer. We would much rather be quiet than add to an overloaded to-do list.

Want to lend your skills?

We are always glad to hear from fellow data enthusiasts who want to make healthcare better for everyone.

Who we are looking for

Fellow data enthusiasts: anyone who enjoys turning a messy spreadsheet into something a busy person can use.

What it is

Volunteers help us offer free data science to community health organizations. The asks are small by design, so tell us what you are good at and how much time you might have.

How to raise your hand

Send us a note through the contact form. Say you would like to volunteer, and tell us a little about your skills.
