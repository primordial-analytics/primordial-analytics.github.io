Principal-led, on purpose.

Primordial Analytics was founded on August 9, 2018. You work directly with Ken Cavagnolo, the founder and data scientist, who does the work himself. There are no hand-offs to juniors, and one accountable person answers for your data. That suits small, high-trust data work. He started out doing client work for people who already knew him.

In 2024 the firm turned its attention to free volunteer data science for non-profit community health organizations, and it kept doing some paid work for longtime clients.

This has been a hard year for the community health organizations we exist to serve. Fewer people asked for our help, which makes sense: even free analytics costs the hours of a person to explain the data and act on it. We wrote about it on our eighth anniversary.

How we try to work

Small is fine

A single chart, a spreadsheet check, or a short call can beat a big project that no one has time to host.

Share what we build

We would like busy health teams to be able to pick up what we make on a Tuesday afternoon, without a kickoff meeting.

No pressure

If your answer is “not right now,” that is the right answer. We would much rather be quiet than add to an overloaded to-do list.

Want to lend your skills?

We welcome volunteers who do end-to-end data science. We are glad to hear from fellow data enthusiasts who want to make healthcare better for everyone.

Who we are looking for

Fellow data enthusiasts: anyone who enjoys turning a messy spreadsheet into something a busy person can use.

What it is

Volunteers help us offer free data science to community health organizations, and the work is the full end-to-end job: framing the question, finding and cleaning data, engineering pipelines, analysis and modeling, visualization, and explaining the results to the people who will act on them. The first asks are small by design, so tell us what you are good at and how much time you might have.

How to raise your hand

Send us a note through the contact form. Say you would like to volunteer, and tell us a little about your skills.
