01

Tell us what you are stuck on

It starts with a note through the contact form and, if it helps, a short call. Tell us the question, who it is for, and what you already have. Messy is fine. Everyone qualifies for free help, pending a quick conflict-of-interest check, and we usually reply within a few business days. Want to tidy your data first? Try our data checklist to run before you report.

  • A short note, or a short call
  • No slide decks or prep
  • “Not right now” is a fine answer
02

Agree on a small ask

We shape the question into something small enough to finish without a big project: a chart, a check, a what-if. If the problem needs the whole job, we can do that too: framing the question, finding and cleaning the data, building the pipeline, analysis and modeling, charts, and explaining the results. We tell you what we would need from your staff before anyone starts.

  • A clear, small deliverable
  • What we need from you, up front
  • How we will handle your data
03

We do the work

We build it as volunteers, checking in with a few quick questions rather than standing meetings. We prefer de-identified or aggregate data, and we will do our best to work within your organization's policies. We generally don't sign BAAs or handle identifiable patient data; in very special cases we're open to it, so ask us.

  • Quick check-ins, not long meetings
  • We will do our best to follow your data policies
  • A sanity check before we send it
04

We hand it over

You get the result with plain notes on how it was made, so your team can keep using it without us. With your okay, we may share the method or tool, without your data, so other teams can use it too.

  • Plain notes, not a binder
  • A walk-through if you want one
  • Something your team can keep using

For paid projects

We still do some paid work for longtime clients. Paid projects have the same shape with more steps: a data and infrastructure audit, design and architecture, iterative development, and deployment with training and handoff. If you have a paid project in mind, get in touch.

Want to see the kind of work this produces? We have worked examples on public data, clearly labeled as examples rather than client projects.
