Who this is for

Primordial Analytics is Ken Cavagnolo, a data scientist in Portland, Oregon. Since 2024 we have offered free volunteer data science to non-profit community health organizations: health centers, free clinics, and small health non-profits.

We built this for small and under-resourced organizations, the ones with a spreadsheet, a deadline, and not enough hours. You do not need a data team, a budget, or a big project in mind. Everyone qualifies for free help, pending a quick conflict-of-interest check (we just want to be sure we are not working for someone on the other side of your question). If you are not sure you are a fit, ask anyway.

We know even free help costs something: the time of a person to explain the data and act on it. That is why the first things below are small on purpose. We also do the full job when it makes sense: framing the question, finding and cleaning the data, building the pipeline, analysis and modeling, charts, and explaining the results in plain words.

Small asks we can take on

Each one is meant to fit into a busy week. These are examples; if your question does not fit, tell us anyway.

A single chart

One clear picture of a number you have been trying to explain to your board, your funders, or your own team.

What we need from you: one short call to understand the question, and access to the numbers behind it.

A spreadsheet or report check

A sanity check of something you have already built, such as a spreadsheet, a dashboard, or a report. We look for numbers that do not add up and tell you what we find.

What we need from you: the file itself, and a few minutes to go over what we found.

A short call

Sometimes the most useful thing is to think a data question through with someone who likes that sort of thing.

What we need from you: a short call to start. Come with the question.

A Medicaid coverage what-if

A simple what-if on your own counts, summarized or de-identified, to get a rough sense of how coverage changes could reach your patients before the dates arrive. Forecasts have wide error bars, and we will say so. Want to try it yourself first? There is a free Medicaid what-if spreadsheet template and a post explaining it, or download the Excel template (.xlsx, about 23 KB).

What we need from you: a short call to agree on the question, then your own counts, ideally summarized or de-identified.

How it works

Three steps, and you can say "not right now" at any point.

01

Tell us

Send a note through the contact form about what you are stuck on. A sentence is enough, and messy is fine.

02

We scope a small ask together

We turn your question into something small enough to finish, and tell you what we would need from your staff before anyone starts.

03

We deliver and hand it over

We do a sanity check before we send it, then give you the result with plain notes on how it was made, so your team can keep using it without us. A walk-through is there if you want one.

How we handle your data

Health data is sensitive, so we keep this simple and careful.

  • We prefer de-identified or aggregate data, such as counts, rates, and totals. Most small asks do not need names or other identifiers.
  • We will do our best to work within your organization's policies. If you have rules about sharing or storing data, tell us at the start.
  • We generally don't sign BAAs or handle identifiable patient data. In very special cases we're open to it, so ask us.
  • Please do not email us patient-level or identifying data. We can work out how to share what is needed before anything is sent.

Questions about any of this are welcome, before you decide to work with us.

Questions you might have

Is it really free?

Yes. This is volunteer work, so we do not charge for it. You do not owe us anything, not even a reply.

How much of my time will it take?

Less than a big project would. Most small asks start with one short call, then a few questions along the way. We will try to tell you up front what we need, so we do not start something you have no time to host.

What if we just have a question?

Send it. Small questions are welcome. If we cannot help, we will say so plainly.

Do you only work in Oregon?

No. We are based in Portland, Oregon, and we work with organizations anywhere, remotely. Everyone qualifies, pending a quick conflict-of-interest check.

Who does the work?

Ken Cavagnolo, the founder and data scientist, does the work himself, so you deal with one accountable person and there are no hand-offs. We welcome volunteers (see the People page), and if someone else works on your project, we will tell you up front. You can read more on the People page.

Can you handle patient data or sign a BAA?

We generally don't sign BAAs or handle identifiable patient data. In very special cases we're open to it, so ask us. For most small asks, counts and totals are all we need.

Who qualifies?

Everyone, pending a quick conflict-of-interest check. We built this for community health organizations, but we will not turn someone away for not fitting a box.

How long until I hear back?

We usually reply within a few business days.

Do you still do paid work?

We still do some paid work for longtime clients. If you have a paid project in mind, get in touch and we will talk it over.
