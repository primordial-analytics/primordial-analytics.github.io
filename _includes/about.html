About Us

Principal-led data science from Portland, free for community health.

2018

We opened our doors

Primordial Analytics was founded on August 9, 2018, by Ken Cavagnolo, a data scientist. The first clients already knew him personally.

2024

We turned to volunteer work

The firm shifted its focus to free volunteer data science for non-profit community health organizations, and kept doing some paid work for longtime clients.

Right Now

Quieter, and still here

It has been a hard year for community health organizations, so we are keeping our help small and light: a chart, a spreadsheet check, a short call.
