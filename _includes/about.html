About Us

A small Portland team that gives its data skills to community health.

2018

We opened our doors

Primordial Analytics was incorporated on August 9, 2018, by a small group of data scientists, engineers, and AI experts. Our first clients already knew us as individuals.

2024

We turned to volunteer work

We shifted our focus to volunteer data science for non-profit community health organizations, and kept serving our longtime clients on a smaller scale.

Right Now

Quieter, and still here

It has been a hard year for community health organizations, so we are keeping our help small and light: a chart, a spreadsheet check, a short call.
