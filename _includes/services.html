Small ways we can help, for free

Specific, small, and free. Pick one, or tell us what you are stuck on.

A single chart

One clear picture of a number you have been trying to explain to your board, your funders, or your own team.

A spreadsheet check

A second pair of eyes on a spreadsheet, dashboard, or report you have already built. We look for numbers that do not add up and tell you what we find.

A short call

Sometimes the most useful thing is talking a data question through with someone. Come with the question; we will tell you if we need anything else.

A Medicaid coverage what-if

A simple what-if on your own counts, summarized or de-identified, to get a rough sense of how coverage changes could reach your patients before the dates arrive.
