Last updated October 22, 2026.

Short version: we count visits in aggregate, we read the messages you send us so we can reply, and we do not sell anything about you. Here are the details.

Visits to this site

We use Google Analytics 4 to see aggregate usage: which pages get read, roughly where visitors come from, and what kind of device they use. Google sets cookies or similar identifiers for this and receives your browser and device information. We use the reports to decide what to write and what to fix. You can block this with your browser's privacy settings, a tracking blocker, or Google's opt-out browser add-on.

The contact form

When you send the contact form, it submits your name, your email, an optional phone number, the option you picked under "I am...", and your message. A form handler hosted on Amazon Web Services sends that to us by email. We use it only to reply to you, and we do not sell or share it.

When the form is sent, the page also tells Google Analytics that a message was sent (a "generate_lead" event) along with the "I am..." choice. It does not include your name, email, phone number, or the text of your message.

Please do not send patient information

Do not put patient names or other identifying details in the form or in email. We generally don't sign BAAs or handle identifiable patient data; in very special cases we're open to it, so ask us first. See how we handle your data.

Other services this site loads

  • Google Fonts serves some of the typefaces. Your browser requests them from Google, which can see your IP address.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare) serves the image lightbox on blog posts, so it loads only on blog posts.
  • LinkedIn, GitHub, and Ken's own website: these are plain links. Nothing from them loads unless you click one.

Each of those services has its own privacy policy, and we do not control what they do with the requests they receive.

Asking us to delete your messages

Email {{ site.email }} and ask us to delete what you sent. We usually reply within a few business days. We will delete the messages from our inbox. We cannot delete what Google Analytics has already collected about site visits, since we have no way to tie it to you.

Changes

If we change how the site works in a way that matters here, we will update this page and its date. Questions are welcome at {{ site.email }}.
