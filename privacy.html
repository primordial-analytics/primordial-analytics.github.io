Visits

We use Google Analytics 4 to see aggregate usage. Google sets cookies and sees your browser and device. You can block this with your browser settings or Google's opt-out add-on.

The contact form

The form sends your name, email, optional phone number, your "I am..." choice, and your message to us by email through a handler hosted on Amazon Web Services. We use it only to reply, and we never sell or share it. Analytics is told that a message was sent, with your "I am..." choice, but not your name, email, phone number, or message.

Patient information

Please don't send patient names or identifying details.

Other services

Google Fonts serves some typefaces, and cdnjs (Cloudflare) serves the image lightbox on blog posts. Each can see your IP address.

Deleting your messages

Email {{ site.email }} and we will delete what you sent. We can't delete analytics data, since we can't tie it to you.
